# Olanrewaju Muili

**PhD Researcher in Reliable AI, Reinforcement Learning, and AI Evaluation**

I study how intelligent systems can make reliable decisions when the information available to them is incomplete, corrupted, uncertain, or misleading.

My research interests span reinforcement learning, AI evaluation, adversarial robustness, uncertainty, interpretability, and reliable decision-making under imperfect information.

---

## Research

### Reliable Decision-Making Under Imperfect Information

My current research investigates how AI agents can assess the reliability of their observations and use that information during sequential decision-making.

I am particularly interested in:

- Reinforcement learning under observation corruption
- Observation reliability and trust calibration
- Adversarial robustness
- Uncertainty estimation and calibration
- AI evaluation and measurement
- Interpretability
- Identifiability and generalization
- Reproducible experimental methodology

Autonomous Python voice-agent system for evaluating AI receptionists through realistic phone-call scenarios, evidence-grounded analysis, and TraceVox-based assurance.

## Current Research Direction

I am developing methods for studying **observation reliability in reinforcement learning**.

The central question is:

> How should an agent behave when it cannot assume that what it observes is trustworthy?

This work studies observation corruption, reliability estimation, sequential decision-making, robustness, and the limits of what can be inferred from imperfect observations.
